- Seats: one table (`lifeos/seats.py`) with failover, one cloud budget and rest-until logic. `delegate` fails over and reroutes a seat that lacks a tool; `ask` does not unless `--failover` is given.
- The privacy guard blocks identifiers, keys, tokens, PINs and tax or social security numbers, also when a token is split over two lines. CI runs it on every tracked file. No personal data lives in this repo; `examples/` is made up.

## Open

- **Windows write error:** reproduced on demand only by racing unlocked writers (fixed). Ten full runs and 8,000 direct writes never fired the retry. Whether that caused every earlier failure is not proven; the weekly soak watches for it.
- **Seat speed on this laptop (`lifeos earn`):** Qwen 4B did not answer in 600 s twice and shows a 262 s median; it waits for the RAM upgrade and a retest. Cloud seats answer in 19 to 55 s.
- **Grok** reads only open pages: shop and price sites block its fetch, and it must answer in its reply, not promise to save.
- **`debate` seats concede, they do not rebut:** three live runs, zero OPEN verdicts, so the evidence gate checks that a pointer exists, not that it is true (`docs/DEBATE.md`).
- The v0.4.0 tag still crashes on `lifeos --help` (a bare percent sign in a help string); use v0.4.1 or later.
- `/usage` cannot be read from inside a session, so the 75 % guard is measured through the seat table instead.
